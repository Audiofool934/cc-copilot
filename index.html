A whole project in view

          

            Follow one session, a selection of sessions, or everything in a
            project. The attention queue brings stalls, repeated failures, and
            open decisions to the top.
          

        

        

          
A separate conversation

          

            Your questions stay in the cockpit. CC-Copilot reads the agent's
            transcript without inserting messages into its working conversation.
          

        

        

          
Use your own model

          

            

              Use your Claude or Codex login, an API provider, or a local Ollama
              model. Model-backed answers send transcript excerpts and project
              evidence to the provider you choose.
            

            

              Basic recaps and status checks work without a model.
              How evidence is handled.

            uv. Open a terminal in your
